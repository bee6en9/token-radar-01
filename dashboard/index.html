    Model constants (radar-2)
    ConstantValueWhat it means

    TVL and seven-day growth come from the snapshot. Age is the time since the protocol was first listed, the best record of how long it has run with no token; an unlisted age uses 365 days. Breadth is the number of chains the protocol runs on: each extra chain up to five adds 3%. The early radar is every pre-token protocol below the watchlist floor, by TVL, and the live-token protocols are set aside with their market caps shown.
